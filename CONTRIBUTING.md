# Contributing to Modularyn

This is the contributing guide for every Modularyn repository that has none of its own.

## Issues are welcome

Bug reports and feature requests help. Open an issue in the repository it concerns and choose the form that fits. Search the open and closed issues first.

A good bug report:

- says what happened, with the exact message or error, and what you expected instead;
- gives the steps to reproduce it, from a fresh checkout where you can;
- names the commit or release, your operating system and the .NET SDK version;
- leaves out secrets, keys, tokens and anything you would not publish.

A good feature request starts from the outcome you want and the situation you need it in, rather than from a particular design, and names the alternatives you considered.

Keep each issue to one problem or one request.

## Outside pull requests are not accepted yet

The terms on which Modularyn accepts outside contributions have not been decided. Until they are, pull requests from outside are not accepted. Open an issue instead, and describe the problem or the change you have in mind.

The source of the paid Standard and Pro modules, published for review in [modularyn-pro](https://github.com/Modularyn/modularyn-pro), takes no outside contributions at all.

## Security

Never report a vulnerability in a public issue or pull request. Report it privately, as the [security policy](SECURITY.md) describes.

## Conduct

Everyone who takes part is expected to follow the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
